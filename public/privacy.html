Privacy Policy

App name: FikaLearn · Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Introduction

At FikaLearn, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, how we keep it secure, and the rights you have over your data. It applies to all users of the FikaLearn mobile app (Google Play package com.flutter_fika_learn) and the website www.fikalearn.com (together, the "Services").

The Services are provided by dVerse Technologies Private Limited, with its registered address at IIT Madras Research Park, Kanagam Road, Taramani, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600113, India ("FikaLearn", "we", "us").

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, email address, mobile number, and password.
  • Payment Information: Billing address, transaction ID, and other payment-related data. Card, UPI and bank details are entered with and processed by our third-party payment gateway; we do not store full card details.
  • Learning Activity: Courses enrolled, progress, quiz scores, and usage analytics.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Support & Communication: Questions, feedback with screenshots, reviews, or messages sent to our support team.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data to:

  • Create and manage your FikaLearn account.
  • Deliver and personalise learning content.
  • Process payments and send invoices or receipts.
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  • Analyse trends to improve app performance and features.
  • Send service messages (OTP, receipts, account and policy notices).

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing of Data

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf, to the extent needed to run the Services, or where the law requires it.

5. Student's Privacy, Children and Minors

FikaLearn is intended for learners aged 10 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 10; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

For users under 18, consent for the collection, use, and processing of data is obtained from a parent or legal guardian during in-app onboarding or through the enrolled institution.

We do not show advertisements to minors, and we do not carry out behavioural tracking or targeted advertising for minors. Analytics are limited to understanding and improving app performance and learning features. A parent or guardian may review, correct or request deletion of their child's data at any time using the contacts in section 11.

6. Data Retention

  • Account and learning data is kept while your account is active.
  • After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.
  • Payment and invoice records are kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law (generally up to 8 years).
  • Support correspondence is kept for up to 24 months after the issue is closed.
  • Backups are overwritten within 90 days.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by email: write to support@dverselabs.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my FikaLearn account".

We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within the period stated in section 6. Deleting your account removes your profile, learning progress and related personal data, except records we must retain by law (for example payment records). Active subscriptions bought through Google Play must also be cancelled in Google Play.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data and account (see section 7).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect processing done before withdrawal. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide parts of the Services.
  • Grievance redressal through the officer named in section 11.

To exercise these rights, email us at the address in section 11.

9. Security of Your Data

We use technical and organisational safeguards such as encryption in transit, access controls, and secure servers to keep your data safe. You should use a strong password and keep it confidential. In case of a data breach, we will inform affected users promptly and comply with all legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at https://www.fikalearn.com/privacy with a new "Last updated" date, and for material changes we will notify you in the app or by email. This version was last updated on 18 November 2026.
